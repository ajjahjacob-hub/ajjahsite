Creative Mentoring & Community Wellbeing

Alongside exhibiting and selling her own paintings, Anna Maria has helped other artists prepare, present and sell their work, accompanied people to exhibitions and delivered practical art tutorials. This community-based, non-clinical creative mentoring grew from a suicide-prevention initiative begun around 2021 and focused on connection, confidence, skill development and supportive pathways forward. Participant identities and personal experiences are not shown.

Art-making was used as a supportive wellbeing practice rather than clinical treatment. Examples of collaborative tutorials are also documented in Collection 12 — Wall & Floor Murals.

Support: In New Zealand, free confidential emotional support is available by calling or texting 1737 at any time. If someone is in immediate danger, call 111.
