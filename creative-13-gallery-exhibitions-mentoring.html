Creative Mentoring & Community Wellbeing

Anna Maria began the suicide-prevention initiative in 2019, while she was also employed by New Zealand Police. In 2021 it developed into a community initiative alongside the establishment of her local art gallery. The gallery became an informal creative drop-in space where people could access materials, explore or rediscover their creative abilities, and receive practical mentoring and tutorials.

The gallery provided a welcoming environment in which participants could build confidence, refine their skills and reconnect with creative practice, including after periods when mental-health challenges had disrupted their creativity. Where appropriate, Anna Maria also supported artists to prepare, present and sell work and accompanied people to exhibitions.

This was community-based creative mentoring and arts-based wellbeing support, not a clinical or therapeutic service. No mentoring participants or personal histories are identified in the current collection.
