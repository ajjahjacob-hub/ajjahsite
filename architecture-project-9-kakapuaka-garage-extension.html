Kakapuaka Garage Extension

← Back to Architecture & Built Projects

Concrete and Timber-Floor Options

This completed garage extension was designed as an addition to an existing garage at Kakapuaka. Two coordinated drawing sets were prepared—one for a concrete slab and the other for a raised timber floor—so the owner could consider both construction approaches before deciding which option to build. The drawings document the proposed layout, elevations, structural sections, foundations, framing and roof junctions.
