PROJECT 1: 2025 Co-Housing Concept Design

Year One · SIT 2025: Completed as first-year academic work.

Completed in 2025, this student project was a collaborative urban co-housing development created by four student groups, involving around 16 students. Each group designed an individual house for a real site in Invercargill. Our team designed the lead house: an upstairs home for a young family above a shared ground-floor living and dining space where the four families could come together. The house was developed collaboratively by the design team pictured below, and I brought the four individual house designs together into the final coordinated development.
