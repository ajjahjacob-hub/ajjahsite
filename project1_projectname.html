Project 1: 2025 Co-Housing Concept Design

Year One · SIT 2025 · Academic team project: The team submission received 99% and was presented collaboratively to an audience. Assessment documentation is available.

This collaborative urban co-housing project brought together four student groups, with each group developing one house for a shared Invercargill site. Our team designed the lead house: a private upstairs home for a young family above communal ground-floor living, dining and kitchen spaces where the four households could gather.

My contribution: For our lead-house proposal, I prepared all floor and site plans, elevations, 3D work, drawings and project images. I also coordinated the four house proposals across the shared site and assembled the final presentation sheets and slides.

Team contribution: My teammates contributed the written material addressing cultural, social and environmental impacts and external cladding. We presented the completed work to an audience as a collaborative team submission.

Team authorship: The architectural drawings and visual material below were prepared and assembled by Anna Maria. The accompanying written contributions are credited to the relevant team members or the wider team, and the 99% result belongs to the team submission.
