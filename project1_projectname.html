Project 1: 2026 Co-Housing Concept Design

SIT 2026 · Academic team project: The team submission received 99%. Assessment documentation is available.

This collaborative urban co-housing project brought together four student groups, with each group developing one house for a shared Invercargill site. Our team designed the lead house: a private upstairs home for a young family above communal ground-floor living, dining and kitchen spaces where the four households could gather.

My contribution: I contributed to the lead-house design and brought the four individual house proposals together into the final coordinated development. The project considered multi-generational living, community connection, privacy, accessibility, shared outdoor space and environmental impact.

Team authorship: Shared documentation is shown to explain the whole submission. Individual sheet authors are credited where identified, and the team material is not presented as Anna Maria’s sole work.
