From Bedroom to Custom Kitchen

This renovation transformed a former bedroom in my own home into a fully functioning kitchen. I stripped the room back to the framing, relined the interior and designed the new layout around the panoramic outlook, everyday workflow and generous storage. The cabinetry was custom-made and fitted to suit the space.
