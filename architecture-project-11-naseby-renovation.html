Naseby Renovation

← Back to Architecture & Built Projects

Design, Renovation and Lived-In Spaces

This hands-on renovation reworked a compact Naseby home around a brighter, more open kitchen, dining and living area. I developed the layout and exterior ideas in CAD and worked directly through demolition, floor rebuilding, framing, lining and finishing. The photographs trace the original interior, the construction process and the later lived-in spaces; the CAD views record a broader veranda concept explored for the property.
