About AJJAH Architecture

Approach & Values

Across three decades of creative, practical and design work, I have developed a careful, people-centred approach to each project. I bring integrity and meticulous attention to every project. My practice focuses on calm, creative spaces—combining design thinking, critical problem-solving and practical buildability so ideas translate clearly into construction.

Anna Maria KOTUA

My background combines architectural technology with extensive experience across art, design, property and hands-on construction. I bring creativity, current technical knowledge and practical understanding to each project, developing thoughtful, buildable residential designs and clear documentation for the building consent process.

Alongside my creative and practical work, I served with New Zealand Police for 28 years (1994–2022). That public-service career strengthened the integrity, careful communication, sound judgement and sense of responsibility I bring to client relationships and project coordination.

Professional note

Anna Maria is completing the New Zealand Diploma in Architectural Technology, with completion expected by the end of 2026. All building-consent documentation prepared by AJJAH is developed under the supervision of a New Zealand Registered Architect. Where required, the supervising architect provides the appropriate Certificate of Design Work. Final building-consent approval is issued by the relevant council.
