Relocatable Glazed Garden Cabin

← Back to Architecture & Built Projects

Deck, Balustrade and Finished Interior

This relocatable glazed cabin was placed in a sunny garden setting. I built the timber deck, balustrade and surrounding timber screen to improve access, safety and usability, creating an inviting outdoor area where its occupants can sit and enjoy the sunshine. I also furnished and dressed the cabin as a comfortable bedroom and living space.
