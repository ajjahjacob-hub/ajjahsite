Extension, Cladding and Access Drawings

This drawing set develops an extension to an existing relocatable house, enlarging the living and dining area and coordinating a new deck and external stair with the existing three-bedroom layout. It records the proposed floor plan, foundation and wall bracing, elevations, construction sections, window and door junctions, and two exterior-cladding phases: plywood sheet cladding followed by brick veneer.
