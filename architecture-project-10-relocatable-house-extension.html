Relocation, Placement and Extension Documentation

This drawing set was prepared to enable an existing three-bedroom house to be relocated onto a new site and extended with a larger living and dining area. The documentation coordinates the placement of the relocated house, new extension, deck and external stair. It includes the proposed floor plan, foundations and subfloor bracing, wall bracing, elevations, construction sections, window and door junctions, and two exterior-cladding phases: plywood sheet cladding followed by brick veneer.
