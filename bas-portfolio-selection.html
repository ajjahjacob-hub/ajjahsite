Practice, leadership & community context

Gallery Practice, Exhibitions & Creative Mentoring

I began a suicide-prevention initiative in 2019 while working for New Zealand Police. In 2021 it became a community initiative alongside the establishment of my local gallery. The gallery combined spatial curation, creative enterprise, public exhibition, practical tutorials and an informal drop-in environment where people could access materials and develop confidence in their creative abilities.

This work demonstrates project initiation, enterprise, circulation and display planning, resource coordination, public presentation, artist support and sustained people-centred leadership. The mentoring was community-based creative support rather than a clinical service.

This supporting context is not counted among numbered works 01–12.
