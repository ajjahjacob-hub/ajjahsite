Residential Design Services

AJJAH provides personal, practical residential design services for new homes, renovations and compact dwellings. Services can be arranged in clear stages—from the first conversation and site information through to developed design and coordinated consent documentation. You'll work directly with me throughout the process, with specialist and licensed professional input arranged where required.

Scope of Services

  • PROJECT PLANNING & SITE INFORMATION: Initial discussions, brief development, site visits, existing building measurements and early consideration of your site, priorities and budget.
  • CONCEPT DESIGN: Plans and design options exploring layout, orientation, room flow, appearance and how the spaces will work for you.
  • DEVELOPED DESIGN: The preferred concept refined through plans, elevations, sections, materials and important construction decisions.
  • BUILDING CONSENT DOCUMENTATION: Clear, coordinated drawing packages prepared for the building consent process with engineering and licensed professional input arranged where required.
  • INTERIORS AND VISUALISATION: Kitchen, bathroom and joinery planning together with three-dimensional views that help you understand the design.
  • PLANNING AND CODE REQUIREMENTS: Early identification of planning considerations and Building Code requirements, with coordination between engineers, surveyors, planners and other specialists where needed.
  • NZBC-ALIGNED DETAILS: Full documentation for council lodgement, including specifications, bracing and lintel schedules, and E2/AS1 weathertightness details where required.
  • RESOURCE CONSENT SUPPORT (IF NEEDED): Planning review, supporting drawings and coordination with planners where required.

Project Types

  • RENOVATIONS & ADDITIONS: Extensions, reconfigurations, sleepouts, garages and decks.
  • NEW HOMES & SMALL DWELLINGS: Practical residential designs shaped around your site and needs.
  • INTERIORS: Kitchen, bathroom and joinery layouts.
  • SMALL COMMERCIAL BUILDINGS: Selected projects considered for concept design.

Pricing & Process

Every project is different. Before work begins, you'll receive a clear proposal outlining the scope, fees and expected timeframe.

  • CLEAR STAGES: Work is divided into manageable steps.
  • AGREED FEES: Costs are explained before each stage begins.
  • REGULAR COMMUNICATION: You'll be kept informed throughout the project.
