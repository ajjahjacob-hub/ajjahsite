Project 7: Commercial Building — Developed Design

Year Two · SIT 2026 · Commercial Building: This is current second-year academic work. Identifying contact and site details have been concealed in the displayed drawings.

This developed-design proposal explores a two-storey multi-tenancy commercial building with workshops, offices, shared circulation, parking, loading access and landscaped outdoor areas. The drawing set coordinates the site strategy, tenancy planning, exterior expression, material systems and building sections.

Project Overview

The cover perspective introduces the overall building form, public frontage, workshop accommodation and relationship to the urban corner site.

Site Planning

These drawings develop building placement, vehicle and pedestrian access, parking, loading, drainage, landscaping and outdoor space.

Floor Plans & Tenancy Planning

The floor plans organise multiple commercial tenancies around workshops, offices, amenities, vertical circulation and shared access.

Exterior Elevations

The elevations develop the relationship between the workshop mass, glazed office frontage, precast concrete, fibre-cement cladding, louvres and landscaped edges.

Building Sections & Construction Strategy

The sections coordinate tenant spaces, workshop structure, concrete floor systems, fire separation, stairs, lift access and the glazed building envelope.
